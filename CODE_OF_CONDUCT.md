# Code of conduct

Savorum discussions require technical candor and professional respect. This
applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Participants must:

- critique proposals and evidence rather than people;
- make room for different levels of technical experience;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and the
  publication of another person's private information; and
- follow maintainer direction when a discussion is moved, narrowed, or closed.

The maintainer may edit or remove abusive material, restrict participation, or
ban participants whose conduct makes collaboration unsafe or persistently
unproductive. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and made without retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to [info@veridorn.com](mailto:info@veridorn.com).
Include relevant links and a short description.
